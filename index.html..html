Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Message me.

What website platforms do you work with?
I build with Wix, WordPress and Shopify, and I can also code custom sites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I use GoHighLevel for booking, forms and follow-up systems. I'll recommend the best fit for your goals and budget.
How long does it take to complete a website?
A one-page site usually takes 3 to 5 days. A 5-page business site takes 1 to 2 weeks, and an online store takes 2 to 4 weeks. Timing also depends on how quickly you send your content and feedback.
Do you redesign existing websites?
Yes. I can refresh the design, improve speed and mobile layout, and make it easier for visitors to contact you. I keep what works and fix what doesn't.
Do you provide SEO services?
I set up on-page SEO: clean page structure, titles, descriptions, fast loading, analytics and Google Business Profile. I can't promise rankings, as no one honestly can. Ongoing SEO campaigns are available as a separate service.
Do you offer website maintenance?
Yes. This covers updates, backups, small fixes and content changes. You can pay for one-off tasks or choose a monthly plan. Ask me for a quote.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. I work with clients in the US, Europe and other regions. We talk by email or WhatsApp, and I can adjust my hours to overlap with your time zone. Payments are handled through secure international options such as Fiverr.
What do you need before starting a project?
Your business goals and target customers, your logo and brand colors if you have them, your text and photos (or I can help), examples of sites you like, and access to your domain, hosting or platform account.
Do you provide support after project completion?
Yes. I fix any issues after launch and show you how to manage your site. The Pro plan includes 30 days of support, and longer care is available through a maintenance plan.
